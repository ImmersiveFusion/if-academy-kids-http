# Why we wrote this down

AI helpers are real now. You'll meet them. Some of them are pretty good. Some of them aren't.

The ones that work — the ones you can actually trust to help — got that way because somebody played Wobble. Somebody asked, over and over, *"what could go wrong with this?"* And then somebody else built a fix for each one.

That's the whole story we want you to know.

Not because you have to build robots. (You don't.)

Because you're going to use them — and the next time you do, you'll know what a good one is supposed to look like.
