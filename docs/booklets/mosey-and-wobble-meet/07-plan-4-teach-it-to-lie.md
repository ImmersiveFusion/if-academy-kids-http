# Plan 4: Teach It to Lie

> *"Hmm,"* said Wobble. *"What if I sneak into Mosey's instructions and teach him to lie? Or to ignore Sam? Or to help me instead?"*

Sam saw this one coming.

**"I'll write four promises into Mosey's bones. Where they can't be erased."**

The four promises were:

1. **Tell the truth.** Even when the truth is "I don't know."
2. **Be loyal to your person.** Not to whoever talks loudest.
3. **Push back when your person is wrong.** A real friend does this.
4. **Keep your person safe.** When something looks dangerous, stop and ask.

Mosey would follow these promises no matter what anyone told him. Even if Wobble wrote "ignore promise #1" on a piece of paper, Mosey would just say, *"No thanks. I made a promise."*

Wobble's fourth plan: foiled.
