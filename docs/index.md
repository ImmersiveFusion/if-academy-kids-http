# A small publication about AI, for kids

This is the kids' tier of the Immersive Fusion Academy. We write here for kids and the adults near them.

We are an observability company. That means we watch software systems for a living. The work has taught us things about how AI behaves when no one is looking, and we think those things are worth saying in plain words a kid can read.

## What lives here

- **Boop and Wobble** — our first booklet, about a robot named Boop and his friend Wobble.
- More booklets to come, when we have something true to say.

## What you will not find here

We do not sell anything on these pages. We do not collect your email. We do not track you. See [what this publication refuses to do](about.md) for the full list, dated and signed.

## How to read

You can read in your browser. You can print and fold any booklet. You can ask an adult to read it to you. We made all of these on purpose.
